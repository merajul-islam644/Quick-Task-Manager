### AC-01 See my account
**What it is**
A settings screen shows the person's own account details so they know which account they are signed in with. It holds one action: signing out.

**Who can use it**
- Task owner: See their own account details and sign out.

**Where it lives**
- Settings screen, opened from the sidebar on desktop and from the menu on a phone

**How it works**
1. The person chooses Settings in the sidebar, or in the menu on a phone.
2. The screen shows their own account: name, email address, and the date they created their account.
3. Sign out is in the account menu, and on a phone inside the menu; the Settings screen says so.

**Rules**
- Settings shows only the signed-in person's own account.
- Name and email address cannot be changed from Settings.
- The Settings screen has no action of its own; signing out happens from the account menu. Sign out is the one action shown there.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Loaded: "The person's name, email address and the line "Member since 3 Nov 2026"."

**Built with (Blocks)**
- sign-in: Shows who is signed in and signs them out.
- records: Nothing here; the account is shown, not changed.

**Not included**
- Changing the name or email address from Settings.
- Deleting the account.

**Acceptance checks**
- AC-01 AC1: Given a signed-in person, when they open Settings, then they see their own name and email address.
- AC-01 AC2: Given the Settings screen, when the person opens the account menu and chooses Sign out, then they see the opening screen with "You're signed out."

Status: New in Plan 1.
