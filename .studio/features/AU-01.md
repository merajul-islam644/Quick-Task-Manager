### AU-01 Sign in and create an account
**What it is**
People sign themselves up or sign in on the app's hosted sign-in page. A new account opens on the dashboard with 3 sample tasks already waiting, so the first visit is never an empty screen.

**Who can use it**
- Visitor: Sign in or create an account from the hosted sign-in page.
- Task owner: Sign in to reach their own dashboard and sign out from the account menu.

**Where it lives**
- Hosted sign-in page
- Sign in or create an account button on the app's opening screen
- Account menu (top right on desktop, inside the menu on a phone)

**How it works**
1. The visitor opens the app and sees the app name and one button, Sign in or create an account.
2. The button sends them to the hosted sign-in page, where they sign in or create an account.
3. The hosted page brings them back to the dashboard, signed in.
4. Sign out sits in the account menu and returns them to the opening screen.

**Other path: Forgotten password**
1. On the hosted sign-in page the person chooses to reset their password.
2. They follow the emailed link and set a new one.
3. They come back to the app and sign in as usual.

**Rules**
- The button on the opening screen reads "Sign in or create an account", because anyone can create an account in this app.
- Signing up, forgotten passwords and setting a new one all happen on the hosted sign-in page; the app has no screens of its own for them.
- A new account opens on the dashboard with 3 sample tasks: Finish project report (done), Read a book (today), Go for a walk (tomorrow).
- Someone who is not signed in never sees a task belonging to anyone.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Empty: "Opening screen: "Quick Task Manager" with "Plan. Focus. Achieve." above the Sign in or create an account button."
- Signed out: "After signing out the person lands back on the opening screen and sees "You're signed out.""

**What it keeps**
- Task: title (required), due date, done (yes/no), owner. Who may: Only the person whose task it is sees, adds, changes and removes it; nobody else in the app can see it.

**Built with (Blocks)**
- sign-in: People sign in on the hosted sign-in page and come back to their own dashboard.
- self sign-up: Anyone can create an account and start using the app straight away.
- records: Keeps each person's tasks, and the 3 sample tasks a new account starts with.

**Not included**
- Social sign-in such as Google or Microsoft.
- A password form inside the app's own pages.

**Acceptance checks**
- AU-01 AC1: Given a signed-out visitor, when they open the app, then they see the button "Sign in or create an account" and one task list is visible to nobody but its owner.
- AU-01 AC2: Given a person who has just created an account, when they land back in the app, then the dashboard lists 3 tasks and the total card reads 3.
- AU-01 AC3: Given a signed-in task owner, when they choose Sign out in the account menu, then they see the opening screen and one button, "Sign in or create an account".

Status: New in Plan 1.
