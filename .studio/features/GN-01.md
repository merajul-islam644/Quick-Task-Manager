### GN-01 Move around on a phone
**What it is**
The same dashboard works on a phone, tablet and desktop. On small screens the fixed sidebar becomes a compact header with a simple menu.

**Who can use it**
- Task owner: Reach Home, Statistics and Settings on a phone, tablet and desktop.

**Where it lives**
- Sidebar on desktop and tablet
- Compact header with a menu on phones
- Every screen of the app

**How it works**
1. On a wide screen the sidebar stays fixed on the left with the app icon, the name Quick Task Manager, Home, Statistics and Settings, a small checklist illustration and the line "Plan. Focus. Achieve."
2. On a tablet the sidebar stays and the cards wrap into fewer columns.
3. On a phone the sidebar is replaced by a compact header holding the app name and a menu button.
4. The menu button opens Home, Statistics and Settings; choosing one opens that screen and closes the menu.

**Rules**
- On a phone the summary cards stack, the task input takes the full width and every card stays one column.
- Buttons and checkboxes stay at least 44 pixels tall so they are easy to tap.
- No screen scrolls sideways at any width.
- Home is selected first and shows as active in the sidebar.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Phone menu open: "Home, Statistics and Settings listed under the app name; the current screen is marked."

**Not included**
- A bottom navigation bar.
- Remembering a chosen filter between visits.

**Acceptance checks**
- GN-01 AC1: Given a phone screen 390 pixels wide, when the person opens Home, then they see a compact header with the app name and a menu button, and no horizontal scrolling.
- GN-01 AC2: Given the compact header on a phone, when the person taps the menu button, then Home, Statistics and Settings are listed and Statistics opens the statistics screen.

Status: New in Plan 1.
