### NU-01 See my statistics
**What it is**
A statistics screen shows live numbers for the person's own tasks: the total, how many are done and how many are pending. The numbers come straight from the task list, so they never disagree with the dashboard.

**Who can use it**
- Task owner: See the counts for their own tasks.

**Where it lives**
- Statistics screen, opened from the sidebar on desktop and from the menu on a phone

**How it works**
1. The person chooses Statistics in the sidebar, or in the menu on a phone.
2. The Statistics screen shows three cards: Total tasks, Completed and Pending, each with its number and a one-line description.
3. Each number is counted from the person's task list at the moment the screen opens, so it always matches the dashboard.
4. A bar under the cards fills with green in the completed share of the total, with the exact line "2 of 5 tasks done" beneath.

**Rules**
- Total tasks counts every task of the person, Completed the done ones and Pending the rest.
- The numbers are worked out from the tasks it shows; nothing is stored separately.
- The completed bar shows The completed share of the total, filled in green.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Empty: "No tasks: all three cards read 0 and the line reads "0 of 0 tasks done"."
- Loaded: "With 5 tasks and 2 done: Total tasks 5, Completed 2, Pending 3, and the line "2 of 5 tasks done"."

**What it keeps**
- Task: title, done, due date, owner (read only to count them). Who may: Only the person whose tasks they are sees these counts.

**Built with (Blocks)**
- records: Counts the person's tasks to show the three numbers.

**Not included**
- Charts over time, streaks or weekly reports.
- Statistics for anyone else's tasks.

**Acceptance checks**
- NU-01 AC1: Given 5 tasks (2 done), when the person opens Statistics, then the three cards read Total tasks 5, Completed 2, Pending 3.
- NU-01 AC2: Given Statistics showing Pending 3, when the person marks another task done on Home, then Statistics reads Pending 2 and Completed 3.
