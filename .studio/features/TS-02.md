### TS-02 Add a task
**What it is**
A person types a task and picks a due date in the Add New Task card at the top of the dashboard. The new task appears at once in the list, pending.

**Who can use it**
- Task owner: Add a task of their own to their list.

**Where it lives**
- Add New Task card on Home, between the summary cards and the task list

**How it works**
1. The person types the task in the input that reads "Enter your task...".
2. They pick a due date with the date field beside it; it starts on today.
3. They press Add Task, or press Enter in the input, and the task appears at the end of the list as Pending.
4. The input clears and stays ready for the next task.

**Other path: Empty or blank task**
1. The person presses Add Task with an empty input or only spaces.
2. Nothing is added; the input shows the hint "Write the task first."

**Rules**
- The task text is required, at most 200 characters; spaces alone do not count.
- A due date is picked with a date field and starts on today; the person can change it to any date.
- Every new task is pending.
- A new task appears in the list and in the summary cards the moment it is added.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Error: "Blank task: "Write the task first.""
- Saved: "The task appears at once as Pending with its date label; no separate confirmation."

**What it keeps**
- Task: title (required, up to 200 characters), due date (required, starts today), done (starts as no), owner (the signed-in person). Who may: Only the person adding it creates it, and only they see it.

**Built with (Blocks)**
- records: Adds the task to the person's list.

**Not included**
- A pop-up window for adding a task.
- Notes, priorities or labels on a task.

**Acceptance checks**
- TS-02 AC1: Given the input holds "Read a book" and the due date is today, when the person presses Add Task, then the list ends with a task reading "Read a book", "Today" and "Pending", and the total card goes up by one.
- TS-02 AC2: Given an input holding only spaces, when the person presses Enter, then nothing is added and they see "Write the task first."
