### TS-04 Edit a task
**What it is**
The person changes a task's text or due date after adding it, and the list shows the new wording and date in place.

**Who can use it**
- Task owner: Edit the text and due date of their own tasks.

**Where it lives**
- Edit action on each task card on Home

**How it works**
1. The person chooses Edit on a task card.
2. The card turns into fields holding the task text and its due date.
3. The person changes the text or the date and chooses Save.
4. The card goes back to its one-line look with the new text and date label, in the same place in the list.

**Other path: Cancel an edit**
1. The person chooses Cancel.
2. The card goes back to its earlier text and date, unchanged.

**Rules**
- The task text is required, at most 200 characters; the save is refused with "Write the task first." when it is blank.
- The due date stays required.
- Saving updates the card in place; a done task stays done and stays in its place.
- The summary cards and tab numbers do not change when only the text or due date changes.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Error: "Blank text: "Write the task first.""
- Saved: "The card shows the new text and date label."

**What it keeps**
- Task: title (required, up to 200 characters), due date (required). Who may: Only the person who owns the task changes it.

**Built with (Blocks)**
- records: Saves the changed text and due date on the task.

**Not included**
- Editing from a separate page.
- Changing a task's owner.

**Acceptance checks**
- TS-04 AC1: Given a pending task reading "Read a book" due Today, when the person edits the text to "Read two books" and the date to tomorrow and saves, then the card reads "Read two books" with "Tomorrow", still Pending, and the counts are unchanged.
- TS-04 AC2: Given a task being edited, when the person clears the text and chooses Save, then they see "Write the task first." and the card keeps its earlier text.
